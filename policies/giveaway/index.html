December Dior giveaway.

Official rules. Last updated November 14, 2026.

Dates

Entries open Tuesday, December 1, 2026 at 10:00 a.m. and close Monday, December 14, 2026 at 11:59 p.m. (Toronto time). Winners are drawn and announced on Tuesday, December 15, 2026.

Who can enter

Anyone aged 18 or older (or the age of majority where they live, if higher), anywhere except the province of Quebec and anywhere this giveaway is prohibited by law, with a public or private Instagram account. Employees of Leruso and their households cannot enter. No purchase is necessary, and buying something does not improve your chances.

How to enter

  • Follow @lerusoeyewear on Instagram and like the giveaway post.
  • Comment on the giveaway post tagging 2 friends. Each comment that tags 2 different friends counts as one entry (no limit, but each friend can be tagged only once).
  • Bonus entry: share the giveaway post to your Instagram story and tag @lerusoeyewear (one bonus entry per person).

Fake accounts, giveaway-only accounts, automated entries or tagging people who are not real friends are disqualified.

Prizes

Two (2) prizes, each one pair of authentic Dior sunglasses chosen by Leruso, approximate retail value up to C$750 each. The models stay a surprise until the winners receive them. One prize per person. Prizes are awarded as is, cannot be exchanged for cash, and are not transferable. Prescription lenses are not included.

Draw and odds

Two winners are drawn at random from all eligible entries on December 15, 2026. Odds depend on the number of eligible entries received. Winners are announced on our Instagram and contacted by direct message from @lerusoeyewear only. We will never ask for payment or card details.

To win, each selected entrant must correctly answer a mathematical skill-testing question without help, and reply within 72 hours. If not, another entrant is drawn.

Pickup

Winners in Ontario pick up their prize in person at our Thornhill boutique by January 31, 2027, with photo ID showing they are 18 or older. Winners outside Ontario get their prize shipped free to their address; Leruso pays shipping, and any customs duties or import taxes in the winner's country are the winner's responsibility.
