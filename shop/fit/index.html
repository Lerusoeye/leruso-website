Your fitting · Order —

Measure your fit.

We place your frame on your face with the camera, check your position point by point, then measure your pupillary distance and your fitting heights for that exact frame. Our optician reviews every measurement before your lenses are made.

1 · Your frame

Choose the frame you ordered.

2 · Before you start

  1. Take your glasses off. Stand facing a window or a bright lamp, so your face is evenly lit.
  2. Hold your phone or laptop camera straight in front of you, at eye level, about an arm's length away.
  3. Look straight into the camera lens. Follow the guides: each check turns gold when it is right.
  4. When everything is gold, hold still for 3 seconds. We take a series of measurements and keep the steadiest.

3 · Your measurements

Mark the card. Drag the two gold lines onto the left and right edges of the card on your forehead.

Your camera image stays on your device. Only your measurements, and the photo if you tick the box, are sent to Leruso. Privacy
